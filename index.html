A Thai Classic: Tom Yum Soup

Ingredients

  • 300 g (10 oz) whole shrimp with heads and shells on
  • 3 cups (750 ml) water
  • 1/2 cup (125 ml) low-sodium chicken stock
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, outer layers peeled
  • 1.5 cm / 3/5 inch piece of galangal
  • 5 kaffir lime leaves
  • 2 Thai or birdseye chilies
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 120 g / 4 oz oyster mushrooms
  • 1 Roma tomato, cut into wedges
  • 1/2 white onion (medium sized), cut into wedges about 1 cm thick
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 3 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 3 tablespoons lime juice
  • Fresh cilantro/coriander, for garnish

Equipment

  • Large pot with a lid
  • Cutting board
  • Sharp knife
  • Meat mallet (or similar) for bruising the aromatics
  • Fine strainer
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Ladle and serving bowls

Directions

  1. Peel the shrimp. Put the heads and shells in the pot and set the shrimp meat aside.
  2. Smash the garlic, chilies, and lemongrass with the mallet so they split open and release their flavor, then add them to the pot.
  3. Crush the kaffir lime leaves by hand and add them to the pot along with the galangal, stock, and water.
  4. Bring to a simmer over high heat, cover, lower the heat to medium, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Strain the broth, throw away the solids, and return the broth to the pot over low heat.
  6. Add the onion and mushrooms and simmer for 3 minutes, then add the tomato and simmer for 1 more minute.
  7. Add the shrimp and simmer for about 2 minutes, until just cooked.
  8. Stir in the sugar and fish sauce and simmer for 1 minute.
  9. Add the lime juice, taste, and adjust the sweetness (sugar), saltiness (fish sauce), and sourness (lime) to your liking.
  10. Ladle into bowls and top with fresh cilantro and chili. Serves 2.
